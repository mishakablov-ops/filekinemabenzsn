# 3D scenes

All 3D blocks are full-frame backgrounds (`"bg": { "block": "…", "props": {…} }`) rendered with three.js (PBR materials, studio environment reflections, soft shadows, ACES tone mapping). They move their own camera — set the scene `"camera": "still"`.
Camera props take a preset name or keyframes `[{ "t": 0, "pos": [x,y,z], "target": [x,y,z], "fov": 30, "ease": "inOut" }, …]` (t in seconds from the scene start).
**Portrait frame:** horizontal FOV is narrow (fov 30 → 17° wide). Wide objects need distance — presets already account for it.

## Devices with LIVE screens

- `IPhone17` stands upright with the camera plateau at the TOP (default rot fixed 2026-10-20) and draws the Dynamic Island over its live screen. `enter: "flip"` rises showing the BACK, then turns 180° onto the screen at `flipAt` (word) over `flipDur`; `float` adds a gentle hover. As a layer over another 3D background: `{"block": "IPhone17", "pos": "full", "enter": "none", "props": {..., "shadow": 0}}` + scene `bg.dim` (0–1, `dimAt`) to darken what's behind. Screen for the channel CTA: `TikTokProfile` (the channel from `engine/src/brand.json`, set by `setup.py --brand`; `highlightAt` frames bio + link; `dark: true` = TikTok's dark theme). `color: "#2C3A4F"` repaints the body (materials named `*color*`) to suit the video's theme. A readable phone screen (chat, profile): camera ~7.5 m, target y ≈ 2.0, fov 30 — the phone fills the lower ¾ under the captions.
The device body is 3D; the screen is real HTML projected onto the 3D display (homography), so any block can run on it, pixel-sharp.
- **Laptop3D** `screen: {block, props}` or `src: "image.png"`; `cam`: `hero | orbit | lowAngle | zoomScreen | top | side | close`; `open: true` (+`openAt`, `openDur`) opens the lid; `color`: `space | silver | midnight | black`; `spin` deg/s; screen HTML is `screenW` (1440) px wide → Terminal `size` 38–42 reads well.
  `zoomScreen` ends with the screen filling the width — cut to a 2D version of the same UI for a seamless "dive into the screen".
- **Phone3D** `screen` (390×844 content: AppScreen, LockScreen, SocialProfile, StoryFrame…), `cam`: `hero | close | low | wide`, `turn: [-18, 8]` degrees over the scene, `enterSpin`, `phones: [{screen, pos, rot, color}]` up to 3 (keep them apart — screens are drawn on top).
- **Model3D** any `.glb` from `engine/public/models/` (see Import below). `screenMesh` + `screen` maps live HTML onto the model's display mesh. `fit` (size), `cam`: `hero | orbit | low | top`, `spin`, `enter: rise | spin`, `anim` plays the embedded clip.

## Places & people (dark pastel low-poly kit)
- **Room3D** cut-away diorama: `preset` `office | living | bedroom | cafe | studio`; `lights` `day | evening | night`; `cam` `diorama | front | closeDesk | pushIn | top`; `people: [{pose, pos, rotY, seed, look, walkTo, walkDur, at}]` (defaults per preset); `screen` = live HTML on the office monitor or living-room TV; `build: true` pops the room up.
- **Street3D** ordinary city street: `time` `day | evening | night`; `cam` `eye | high | follow | crossing | top | drone`; `cars`, `people` (walkers use dedicated lanes — they never pass through trees, lamps or each other), `traffic: red` stops one lane, `highlight: {x, z, label, at}`.
  «Everyone vanished» kit (2026-10-26, posledniy-chelovek): `people: 0, stopped: true` — cars stand where they stopped, every other driver door open; `lightsOut: "@word"` — windows, lamps and headlights die out over ~1.2 s (negative seconds = already dark); `stars: true` — night sky with a Milky Way band (look up: target y ≫ camera y); `wolves: [{x, z, speed, dir, seed}]` — low-poly wolves trotting down the road; `walkers[].rotY` / `pose` for a standing hero.
- **Scene3D** build your own: `objects: [{type, pos, rotY, at, …}]` with types person · desk · chair · monitor · laptop · sofa · bed · table · shelf · plant · lamp · rug · frame · mug · pendant · counter · stool · tv · car(drive) · tree · streetlamp · bench · traffic · building · box; `labels: [{text, pos, at}]`; `cam` keyframes; `ground`.
- **People** poses: `stand | idle | walk | run | sit | type | wave | point | think | phone | cheer`; `look: {skin, hair, shirt, pants, hairStyle: short|long|bun|bald|cap}` or a `seed`. Seated poses: put them on a chair/sofa at hip height 0.45–0.5.
- Collision QA: standing/walking people must not intersect furniture, walls or other people — `stills.mjs --qa` logs `collisions3d`.

## Worlds
- **City3D** grows from the ground: `look` `warm | white | night | clay`, `cam` `orbit | dive | street | iso | close`, `blossom` (share of pink trees), `downtown` (tower height), `highlight`.
- **Globe3D** real coastlines: `look` `dots | solid | night | light | dusk` (warm dark Earth for the dusk palette); `cities: true` warm night lights of ~140 big cities, `citiesOut: "@word"` switches them off one by one within ~1.5 s; `dist` 4–4.6 keeps the whole limb + atmosphere in the 9:16 frame; `focus: [{t, lat, lon, dist}]` flies the camera; `markers: [{lat, lon, label, at}]`; `arcs: [{from: [lat,lon], to: [lat,lon], at, dur}]`.

## Importing real models (BlenderKit, Sketchfab, Poly Haven, your own)
1. In Blender install the **BlenderKit add-on** (Edit → Preferences → Add-ons → Install from disk → the `blenderkit-v*.zip`), log in, search e.g. "MacBook" / "iPhone", download (free assets are fine for videos; check each asset's licence).
   The add-on stores downloads in `~/blenderkit_data/models/…/*.blend`.
2. Convert everything found there (or any folder / file: .blend .fbx .obj .glb .usdz):
   `$PY $RS/scripts/import_models.py [path]` → `engine/public/models/<name>.glb` + `<name>.json` (meshes, per-material sizes, guessed `screenMaterial` / `screenMesh`). Blender runs with `--disable-autoexec` (scripts embedded in downloaded .blend files never run). Keep originals in `assets/models-src/` (not in `public/`, it gets bundled).
3. Use it: `{ "block": "Model3D", "props": { "src": "models/<name>.glb", "screenMaterial": "<from json>", "rot": [-1.5708, 0, 0], "screen": { "block": "Terminal", "props": {…} }, "cam": "orbit" } }`.
   `rot` is applied before measuring, so a model lying flat stands on the floor. Corner order is derived in world space (never mirrored); a display facing away from the camera hides its HTML. `screenRadius` rounds the live screen. If a model has no separate display material, use the procedural Laptop3D/Phone3D.
Large models: `blend2glb.py … --decimate 0.5`. Keep each GLB < 40 MB.

## Performance
3D scenes render ~3–8 fps on this machine at 1080×1920. Keep Street3D people ≤ 16 and cars ≤ 8, City3D blocks ≤ 8, Terrain one per video. Previews at 30 fps.

## Points (stars, city lights, dust)

`THREE.PointsMaterial` without a `map` draws square points. Use `map: dotTexture()` from `three/core.tsx` (a round soft sprite);
a glow = the same geometry drawn again with a big `size` and low `opacity` (additive).
